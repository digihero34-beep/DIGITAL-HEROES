## Brand & Style

The brand evokes the rarefied atmosphere of storied golf clubhouses, championship archives, and sovereign trust ledgers. It rejects vulgar lottery tropes, frenetic casino gamification, and ephemeral tech gradients in favor of an institutional, high-credibility ethos: the mathematical transparency of modern fintech married to the architectural dignity of classic athletic philanthropy.

The audience consists of discerning patrons, philanthropic sportsmen, and modern golf enthusiasts who demand absolute regulatory confidence, audit clarity, and an elevated editorial presentation. Visual cues rely on strict rectilinear geometries, rigorous editorial baseline grids, whisper-thin borders, and classical serif typographics. The UI delivers an air of solemn prestige, enduring heritage, and calm, undeniable legitimacy.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unshakeable air of traditional heritage framed with modern precision:

- **Primary (`#132E24`)**: Deep Augusta forest green. Carries the ultimate structural gravity for master navigation, dominant action containers, and authoritative displays.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27`)**: Muted championship ochre gold. Reserved for verified hallmarks, key financial markers, draw progression indicators, and prestige highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#234E3E`)**: British racing spruce. Applied to interactive focus states, secondary buttons, and structured status tags.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#FBF9F5`)**: Warm archival vellum. Replaces harsh synthetic whites to deliver warm paper-weight credibility.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Crisp alpine card backing, providing sharp contrast against the cream ground.
- **Text Body & Data (`#1A2320`)**: Deep slate charcoal, maximizing legibility without the jarring starkness of pure digital black.
- **Border & Rule Accent (`#E2EAE6`)**: Subtle sage-tinted hairline rules that reinforce architectural grid boundaries.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the classical, literary grace of **EB Garamond** with the technical clarity and rhythmic legibility of **Manrope**.

- **Editorial Serifs (`EB Garamond`)**: Used exclusively for tier-one headlines, grand prize narratives, and philanthropic proclamations. Set with tight line height and deliberate letter-spacing to emulate broadsheet pedigree and historic club publications.
- **Precision Sans (`Manrope`)**: Powers all transactional surfaces, entry calculations, charity audit stats, time indicators, and regulatory disclosures.
- **Labels & Metrology**: Micro-labels are strictly transformed to uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`), producing crisp, ledger-like precision across all draw statistics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column grid anchored by generous borders and white space.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Maximum structured content container maxes at 1280px to preserve editorial balance.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px canvas margins. Secondary metrics collapse from multi-column tables into dual-column data manifests.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px outer margins. Complex ledger comparisons stack strictly into flush vertical cards.

Component spacing obeys rhythmic multiples of 4px and 8px, prioritizing wide internal card breathing room (`space-xl`) to convey unhurried elegance and wealth of scale.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews deep blurred drop shadows and float abstractions. Depth is governed strictly through physical editorial layers and sharp containment lines:

- **Surface Tiers**: The base application sits on archival vellum (`#FBF9F5`). Interactive modules, entry manifests, and prize dossier blocks use crisp white tiles (`#FFFFFF`) or deep racing green slabs (`#132E24`).
- **Hairline Framing**: Visual boundaries are maintained via 1px crisp borders using `#E2EAE6` on light surfaces or `#234E3E` on dark structural plates.
- **Micro-Layering**: Hover and focus transitions rely on border-color shifts to warm ochre (`#C59B27`) or subtle 1px inner keylines rather than spatial movement or diffuse elevation.

## Shapes

The shape grammar is purely rectilinear (`roundedness: 0`). 

Every element—including buttons, input containers, image viewports, progress meters, and modal dialogs—has a crisp 0px border radius. This razor-sharp visual language recalls vintage stock certificates, printed scorecards, fine bookbinding, and bespoke financial reports, instantly distinguishing the platform from consumer gaming apps.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: Solid `#132E24` background, pure white text, 0px radius, 1px border of `#132E24`. Generous vertical and horizontal padding (`14px 28px`), `label-lg` uppercase typography. Active state transitions to `#234E3E`.
  - *Secondary*: Transparent background, 1px solid `#132E24` border, `#132E24` text. On hover, fills with subtle cream wash.
  - *Prestige Action*: Solid `#C59B27` ochre gold, charcoal text (`#1A2320`), for final entry confirmation.
- **Inputs & Fields**:
  - Inset rectangular boxes with 1px `#E2EAE6` borders, white ground, and `#1A2320` text. Focused fields activate a sharp 1px `#132E24` border with no diffuse outer glow.
- **Chips & Status Pills**:
  - Rectangular micro-badges (sharp corners) featuring a 1px border. Verified charity and regulatory tags display a subtle `#234E3E` border with forest green type. Live draw tags use `#C59B27` framing.
- **Cards & Prize Dossiers**:
  - Pure white rectangular planes bordered with 1px `#E2EAE6`. Hero prize dossiers incorporate a 2px top rule in `#C59B27`. Internal content relies on generous `space-lg` and `space-xl` separation.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Exact squares with 1px slate borders. Check states use a solid `#132E24` fill featuring a sharp geometric checkmark icon; radios feature an inner square token.
- **Ledger Tables & Transparency Meters**:
  - Full-width rule lines with 1px `#E2EAE6` divisions, monospaced tabular numbers for odds and charity fund splits, reinforced with `metric-display` figures.
